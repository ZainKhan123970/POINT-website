OUR STORY

Street-Born.
Built To Last.

Where It Started

POINT began on the streets of Karachi — not in a studio, but in the sketchbooks and spray cans of a small crew who thought local streetwear deserved the same weight as anything coming out of Tokyo or LA.

The name says it all: every piece has to earn its place. No filler drops, no rushed prints — just tees built heavy, cut oversized, and finished with a mark you'd recognize from across the room.

The Mark

The graffiti wordmark with the dripping halo is deliberate — a nod to the brand's street roots, and the "elevated" standard we hold every piece to. It's screen-printed in gold on every tee, never a sticker, never a shortcut.

What launched as a single tee drop is growing one product at a time — but the standard stays the same: premium heavyweight cotton, oversized fits, and a print that outlasts the shirt.
